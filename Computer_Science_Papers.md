# Foundational Paper in Computer Science

Turing (1936) — On Computable Numbers, with an Application to the Entscheidungsproblem. Computability and the universal machine. ⚠️ Most copies are scans; needs OCR.
Shannon (1948) — A Mathematical Theory of Communication. Entropy, channel capacity, the bit. The Bell Labs reprint PDF has selectable text.
Cook (1971) — The Complexity of Theorem-Proving Procedures. Birth of NP-completeness. Pair it with Karp (1972) if you want the reduction zoo.

Programming & software design
4. Dijkstra (1968) — Go To Statement Considered Harmful. Two pages, still argued about.
5. Hoare (1969) — An Axiomatic Basis for Computer Programming. Pre/postconditions; the root of formal verification.
6. Parnas (1972) — On the Criteria To Be Used in Decomposing Systems into Modules. Information hiding — the most quietly influential paper on this list.

Data & systems
7. Codd (1970) — A Relational Model of Data for Large Shared Data Banks. Every SQL database traces here.
8. Cerf & Kahn (1974) — A Protocol for Packet Network Intercommunication. TCP/IP.
9. Dean & Ghemawat (2004) — MapReduce: Simplified Data Processing on Large Clusters. Clean PDF, easy ingest.

Distributed systems
10. Lamport (1978) — Time, Clocks, and the Ordering of Events in a Distributed System. Happens-before, logical clocks.
11. Fischer, Lynch & Paterson (1985) — Impossibility of Distributed Consensus with One Faulty Process. The FLP result. Short and brutal.

Security
12. Diffie & Hellman (1976) — New Directions in Cryptography. ⚠️ Common scans are poor quality; find the IEEE reprint.
13. Rivest, Shamir & Adleman (1978) — A Method for Obtaining Digital Signatures and Public-Key Cryptosystems. RSA.

Machine learning
14. Krizhevsky, Sutskever & Hinton (2012) — ImageNet Classification with Deep Convolutional Neural Networks. AlexNet; the moment deep learning took over.
15. Vaswani et al. (2017) — Attention Is All You Need. On arXiv, clean text layer.
